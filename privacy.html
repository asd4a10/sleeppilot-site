Privacy Policy

SleepPilot for iOS — effective 23 July 2026

Summary

SleepPilot does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. Everything the app knows lives on your device.

Health data

With your permission, SleepPilot reads and writes sleep data through Apple HealthKit to show your sleep history and record sleep sessions. This data never leaves your device and is never shared with us or with any third party. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Screen Time and app blocking

SleepPilot uses Apple's Family Controls (Screen Time) framework to shield apps you choose during your sleep window. Apple's design keeps your app selection opaque to us: SleepPilot cannot see which apps you pick, and nothing about your usage is collected or transmitted.

Journal and settings

Your sleep journal entries, schedule, and preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third parties

SleepPilot contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or analytics tools and makes no network requests with your data.

Children

SleepPilot does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes
