Privacy Policy

SleepPilot for iOS — effective 29 July 2026

Summary

SleepPilot does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, and we operate no server that receives your information. Everything the app knows about your sleep lives on your device. The app makes exactly one kind of network request — fetching our announcements file — and it sends nothing about you when it does. That is described below.

Health data

With your permission, SleepPilot reads and writes sleep data through Apple HealthKit to show your sleep history and record sleep sessions. This data never leaves your device and is never shared with us or with any third party. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Screen Time and app blocking

SleepPilot uses Apple's Family Controls (Screen Time) framework to shield apps you choose during your sleep window. Apple's design keeps your app selection opaque to us: SleepPilot cannot see which apps you pick, and nothing about your usage is collected or transmitted.

Journal and settings

Your sleep journal entries, schedule, and preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Announcements

The Inbox in Settings shows announcements from us — release notes, tips, and occasional offers. To display them, the app downloads a small public file from this website. The request is anonymous: it contains no account, no device identifier, and nothing about your sleep, your schedule, or your settings. We cannot tell who fetched it, and we do not know whether you opened a message. Which messages you have read is recorded only on your device.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives your device's IP address when the file is downloaded, exactly as it would for any web page you visit. GitHub's handling of that is covered by their privacy statement. We receive nothing.

Third parties

SleepPilot contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or analytics tools. It never transmits your data to us or to anyone else. The only network request it makes is the anonymous announcements download described above.

Children

SleepPilot does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.
