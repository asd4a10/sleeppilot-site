Frequently asked questions

Where is my sleep data stored?
Everything stays on your device. SleepPilot reads and writes sleep data through Apple HealthKit and never sends it anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How does app blocking work?
SleepPilot uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to shield the apps you choose during your sleep window. You can change or disable the selection at any time in the app's settings.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes all of its local data. Sleep samples saved to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app under Browse → Sleep.
