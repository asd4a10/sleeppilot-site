# Welcome aboard

SleepPilot moves your sleep schedule **gradually** rather than all at once. Each night your target shifts a little closer to your goal, anchored to where you *actually* sleep — so it stays realistic instead of becoming a number you ignore.

## Your first week

- Set a goal bedtime and wake time in the Schedule tab
- Start a sleep session each night so sounds are recorded
- Check the journal each morning — sleep is filed under the day you wake up

Don't worry about hitting the goal straight away. Consistency matters more than any single night, and the plan is built to move at a pace you can actually keep.

## A note on privacy

Your sleep data stays on your device. There's no account and no server collecting anything about you.
